Lyrics Companion

Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Lyrics Companion is a personal app for Android (and later Windows and Mac) that shows lyrics for the song playing and keeps listening stats. It has no server of its own: what it knows about you stays on your devices, and in your own Google Drive if you connect it. Nothing is sent to the developer, sold, or used for ads.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. When you connect, the app asks only for the drive.appdata permission, which gives it a hidden folder in your Drive that only this app can see. It can't see or change any of your other files.

In that folder the app keeps, for each of your devices:

  • the songs you played while the app was running: title, artist, album, length, when you listened and for how long;
  • your Spotify streaming history, if you imported it in the app.

Your other devices read these files so your stats include everything you played. The app uses this data only for that.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

  • To find lyrics, the app sends a song's title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB.
  • If you sign in to Spotify, the app uses Spotify's API to read your playlists, saved songs and top tracks.

These services' own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect in the app under Settings › Sync.
  • To delete the app's folder from Drive: open Google Drive on the web, then Settings › Manage apps › Lyrics Companion › Options › Delete hidden app data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes what it kept on that device.
